## Something on a site stopped working

Chat sites redesign their pages from time to time. Sidenote usually repairs itself within a day (Settings › General › *Check now* does it immediately). If a site still misbehaves:

1. Turn hidden mode off (⌘⇧D by default), open the site, send one message.
2. Click **Copy diagnostics** in the bar at the bottom of the window.
3. Email the pasted text to the address above. It describes the page's structure only; none of your messages are included.

## Common questions

**Where are my logins stored?** On your Mac only, in the app's own sandboxed storage. *Settings › Privacy › Sign out of all sites* removes them.

**Does the app send my conversations anywhere?** No. The only network traffic is to the site you open, plus a daily download of a small compatibility file from this repository.

**A shortcut doesn't work.** Another app may own that key combination. Pick a different one in *Settings › Shortcuts*; a single function key (F8, F9…) rarely conflicts.

**The site asks me to sign in again.** Sign‑in pages open normally (hidden mode doesn't apply to them). Complete the sign‑in; the chat appears disguised afterwards.

**Sites are blocked on my network.** Sidenote uses your Mac's normal internet connection and does not route around network restrictions.
