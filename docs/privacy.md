# Sidenote, Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 27, 2026*

Sidenote is a desktop client for AI chat services you already use. It is built so that **no data about you is collected by the app or its developer.**

## What the app does with data

- **Your sign‑ins and conversations** stay between you and the service you open (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Perplexity). Sidenote shows that service's own website inside the app. Cookies and site data are stored on your Mac in the app's private, sandboxed web storage, exactly as a browser would store them. You can delete them at any time with *Settings › Sign out of all sites*.
- **Settings** (your chosen style, shortcut, customisation text) are stored on your Mac only.
- **Hidden mode** rearranges what the service's page shows on your screen. It runs entirely on your Mac. The text it displays is read from the page already in front of you; nothing is sent anywhere else.
- **Diagnostics** are produced only when you press *Copy diagnostics*. They are placed on your clipboard and nowhere else. They describe page structure and contain no message text.



## What the app sends over the network

Only requests to the services you choose to open, under their own terms, plus a request to each service's website for its icon on the workspace list. The maths renderer (KaTeX) is built into the app and loads nothing from the internet. Once a day the app may download a small configuration file from the developer's own server (it describes how each service's web page is laid out, so the app keeps working when a service redesigns). That request carries no information about you; it is a plain file download. Sidenote has no servers, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no crash reporting.

## What the developer receives

Nothing. The developer has no way to see your account, your conversations, your settings, or whether you use the app at all.

## Third‑party services

Each service you open is governed by its own privacy policy and terms of use. Sidenote is independent and is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft or Perplexity.

## Children

Sidenote is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
