# Sidenote — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

Sidenote is a Mac app for AI chat services you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity). It is built so that **no data about you is collected by the app or its developer.** Everything Sidenote stores stays on your Mac, and, only if you use iCloud backups, in your own iCloud Drive.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Sign‑ins and site data.** Sidenote shows each service's own website inside the app. Cookies and site data are kept in the app's private, sandboxed web storage on your Mac, as a browser would keep them.
- **Sign‑in status.** To show "Signed in" on the workspace page, Sidenote checks whether a service's sign‑in cookie exists and whether the page shows a "Sign in" button. Only the cookie's *name* is checked; its value is never read. For a service without a recognisable sign‑in cookie (Copilot), Sidenote briefly opens that service's page invisibly when the app starts, only if you have used it in Sidenote before, to see whether it asks you to sign in; nothing is clicked or typed, and the page is closed straight away.
- **Kept answers and projects.** When you keep an answer, its question, answer, service, date and conversation address are saved in a file in the app's sandboxed storage on your Mac. Pictures and files that came with the answer are downloaded from that service, under your own sign‑in, and stored beside it in the same storage. They are deleted with the answer.
- **Highlights, notes, stars and edits.** Highlights and notes you add to kept answers, notes you write in projects, stars, edits and trims you make, and the earlier text kept so edits can be undone, are stored in the same file. Clean‑up of new answers runs on your Mac (with Apple's on‑device Foundation Models when Apple Intelligence is on); nothing is sent anywhere.
- **The information network and summaries.** Sidenote analyses kept answers **on your Mac** with Apple's on‑device frameworks (NaturalLanguage and, when Apple Intelligence is turned on, Apple's on‑device Foundation Models). Nothing is sent to Apple, the developer or any other company for this.
- **Search** across kept answers runs on your Mac.
- **Settings** (styles, shortcuts, appearance, window options) are stored on your Mac.
- **Hidden mode** changes how a service's page looks on your screen. It runs entirely on your Mac; the text it shows is read from the page already in front of you.
- **Diagnostics** are created only when you press *Copy diagnostics*, and are placed on your clipboard only. They describe the page's structure and contain no message text.
- **Exports and backups** (Markdown, PDF, Word, RTF, print, and project backup files) are written only where you choose. Restoring a backup with *Replace* first keeps a copy of your current projects in the app's own storage.

## iCloud backups (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on, Sidenote backs up your projects, kept answers (with their highlights, notes, edits and stored pictures) and its own settings once a day, and when you choose *Back Up Now*, to **your own iCloud Drive**, in a container that only Sidenote uses. The last 10 backups from each Mac are kept. They are stored and synced by Apple under your Apple Account and the iCloud terms; the developer has no access to them. Sign‑ins to the AI services are never included. Turn this off in *Settings › Backups*; delete the backups in *System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage › Sidenote*.

## What is sent over the network

- **To the AI service you open, under your own account.** Whatever you type into a service — including through hidden mode's input box — is sent to that service, exactly as if you used its website in a browser. That service's own privacy policy and terms apply. Sidenote does not send your conversations anywhere else.
- **A configuration file from the developer's site.** Once a day (or when you press *Check now*), Sidenote may download a small file describing how each service's web page is laid out, so it keeps working after a redesign. It contains no code, and the request carries no information about you.

- **The App Store, for purchases.** The free week and the one‑time unlock are in‑app purchases handled entirely by Apple. Sidenote asks Apple which of them your Apple Account owns, to unlock features; it never sees your payment details, and nothing about purchases is sent to the developer beyond the sales reports Apple provides to every developer.

Sidenote has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.

## What the developer receives

Nothing. The developer cannot see your accounts, conversations, kept answers, notes, backups, settings, or whether you use the app.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it:

- **Kept answers:** delete a project in the Projects window (its answers are deleted with it).
- **Sign‑ins and site data:** *Settings › Privacy › Sign out of all sites*, or right‑click a workspace › *Sign Out*.
- **iCloud backups:** *System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage › Sidenote* (turn automatic backups off in *Settings › Backups*).
- **Everything:** delete the app. macOS then removes its sandboxed storage, including kept answers, settings and site data. iCloud backups, if any, stay in your iCloud until you delete them as above.

## Third‑party services

Each service you open is run by its own company under its own privacy policy and terms of use. Sidenote is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft or Perplexity.

## Children

Sidenote is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
