# Sidenote — Support

**Email:** thelittlebraincompany@gmail.com

Questions about the app, the free week or a purchase: write any time. Replies usually come within two working days.

## Something on a site stopped working

Chat sites redesign their pages from time to time. Sidenote usually repairs itself within a day (Settings › General › *Check now* does it immediately). If a site still misbehaves:

1. Turn hidden mode off (⌘⇧D by default), open the site, send one message.
2. Click **Copy diagnostics** in the bar at the bottom of the window.
3. Email the pasted text to the address above. It describes the page's structure only; none of your messages are included.

## Free week and unlock

**How does the free week work?** Everything is free for 7 days from the moment you tap *Start Free Week*. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks hidden mode, the boss key and keeping new answers for good. There is no subscription and nothing is charged automatically.

**What stays free after the week?** Using your AI services in normal mode, and reading, searching, exporting and backing up everything in Projects.

**I bought the unlock on another Mac (or reinstalled).** Open *Settings › Unlock* and click **Restore Purchases**, then sign in with the Apple Account you paid with. The unlock works on every Mac signed in to that account.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple: go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in, and choose the purchase.

## Projects and backups

**Are my projects backed up?** If you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on, Sidenote backs up every project, kept answer, highlight, note and picture to your own iCloud Drive once a day (*Settings › Backups*), keeping the last 10 from each Mac. *Back Up Now* makes one straight away. Backups are free, with or without the unlock.

**Can I move my kept answers to another Mac?** Yes. With iCloud: on the new Mac, *Settings › Backups › Restore…*, pick a backup from your other Mac, and choose **Merge** to keep what's already there (tick *Also restore settings* to bring your hidden mode styles and shortcuts too). Without iCloud: *File › Back Up Projects…* saves everything to one file; on the other Mac, *File › Restore Projects from Backup…*.

**How do I highlight or add a note?** Open an answer in Projects, select some text and pick a colour, *Note* or *Delete Text*. Click a highlight to change its colour, add a note or remove it. Notes can be hidden to a small ✎ marker and shown again. Exports and prints include highlights and notes unless you turn off *Include Highlights & Notes* in the export menu.

**Can I choose my own highlighter colours?** Yes: *Settings › Projects › Highlighters*. Pick a colour for each of the five highlighters, give it a meaning ("Key point", "Check this") and a key. Select text in an answer and press the key (1–5 by default, N for a note).

**Where do I find everything I highlighted?** Each project has a highlighter tab listing its highlights and notes, filterable by colour; choosing one shows it in its answer. *Highlights & Notes* in the sidebar lists them across all projects. *Starred* collects answers you star (☆ next to an answer's date), and *Recently Kept* the last month's answers.

**Why does Projects say "Source A" instead of ChatGPT?** So nothing on screen says which AI an answer came from. Choose your own names, or switch the real names back on, in *Settings › Projects › Service names*.

**How do I edit a kept answer?** Hover over the answer and click *Edit*. You edit it right where it is, with your highlights and notes still showing. Use B, I, U and S (or ⌘B, ⌘I, ⌘U, ⇧⌘X) for bold, italic, underline and strikethrough, then *Save* (⌘↩) or *Cancel* (Esc). Formulas, tables and pictures stay intact.

**Can I undo a highlight or a note?** Yes. Press ⌘Z in the answer, click *Undo* next to *Edit*, or use the ↩︎ button at the top of the reading panel. It undoes the last highlight, colour change, note or edit on that answer.

**Can I write my own notes in a project?** Yes: open the project's notes tab (the note icon) and click *New Note*. Your notes are searchable, included when you export or print the project, and backed up with it.

**What does clean-up do?** With *Settings › Projects › Clean up answers when you keep them* on, filler such as "Great question!" or "Let me know if you have other questions" is removed as an answer is kept. Whole sentences are removed and nothing is reworded.

**Can I undo a merge or a split?** Yes: use *Undo* in the message that appears, or the ↩︎ button at the bottom of the projects list.

**The network view is empty or still spinning.** Each answer is analysed on your Mac after it's kept; a large restore can take a few minutes. With Apple Intelligence turned on, concepts and their relations are added too.

## Hidden mode

**The site shows its own sign-in page instead of hidden mode.** Each service needs to be signed in once. Hidden mode steps aside on a sign-in page so you can use it, and turns on as soon as you're signed in.

**A reply doesn't appear in hidden mode.** Turn hidden mode off for a moment to check the site itself finished replying, then follow the steps in "Something on a site stopped working" above.

**It says I'm offline.** Sidenote reconnects by itself when the network is back. Anything you typed while offline stays in the box; press Return again to send it.

## Common questions

**Where are my logins stored?** On your Mac only, in the app's own sandboxed storage. *Settings › Privacy › Sign out of all sites* removes them.

**Does the app send my conversations anywhere?** No. The only network traffic is to the site you open, plus a daily download of a small compatibility file from this repository.

**A shortcut doesn't work.** Another app may own that key combination. Pick a different one in *Settings › Shortcuts*; a single function key (F8, F9…) rarely conflicts.

**The site asks me to sign in again.** Sign‑in pages open normally (hidden mode doesn't apply to them). Complete the sign‑in; the chat appears disguised afterwards.

**Sites are blocked on my network.** Sidenote uses your Mac's normal internet connection and does not route around network restrictions.
